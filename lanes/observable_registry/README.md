# Observable registry

The [scalar-glueball lane](../glueball_spectroscopy/README.md) supplies executable
2+1-dimensional SU(2) definitions for the plaquette and four larger rectangular
loops. Its [registry](../glueball_spectroscopy/artifacts/operators.json) records
Tr/2 normalization, zero-momentum D4/A1 projection, vacuum subtraction, spatial
volume normalization and per-link oscillation envelopes.

The A1 label is a lattice representation, not a certified continuum spin or
single-particle identification. These definitions do not certify observable
completeness in a physical transfer Hilbert space. Wilson-loop and Polyakov-loop
registries outside this scalar subset remain future work.
